🛡️ Safety Nets (Every 5 Stages)

Reaching Stage 5, 10, 15... 95 locks in a Safety Net checkpoint. Your highest ever stage reached is Stage 1.

⚠️ Greed & Defeat Penalty

If you are defeated between safety nets (e.g., Stage 13), you are knocked back to your previous Safety Net (Stage 10), lose unbanked training gains, and suffer a -2% to -5% base stat penalty!

♻️ Cash Out & 10% Repeat Decay
